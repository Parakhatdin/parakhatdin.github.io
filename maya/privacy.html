Privacy policy

Maya Tries Tools Uploader · last updated 31 October 2026

Maya Tries Tools Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Maya Tries Tools to publish that channel's videos. It is not offered to other users.

What it accesses

  • With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), it uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's channel, and to read that channel's own statistics, analytics and comments.
  • It does not access any other person's Google account or data.

How the data is used and stored

  • Channel statistics and comments are used only to decide when to publish the next video and which tools viewers ask for. They are processed in short-lived sessions and are not sold or shared with anyone.
  • The access credentials are stored as private secrets in the owner's own environment and are never published.

Third parties

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at Google account permissions, or through the Google security settings.
