The Human FAQ

A YouTube channel (@TheHumanFAQ): everyday questions about being human, answered with stick figures. What if you never slept? Why do we eat three meals a day? How would you survive a week in a medieval village?

Every episode is researched from real scientists, historians and studies, listed in each video's description. The illustrations and the narrator's voice are made with AI tools; the scripts and sources are checked by the channel owner.

The Human FAQ Studio Uploader is the channel's own publishing tool. It uses the YouTube API Services to upload the channel's videos and thumbnails and to read the channel's own statistics. It is used only by the channel owner.
