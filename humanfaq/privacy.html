Privacy policy

The Human FAQ Studio Uploader · last updated 1 November 2026

The Human FAQ Studio Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Human FAQ to publish that channel's videos. It is not offered to other users.

What it accesses

  • With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), it uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's channel, and to read that channel's own statistics and analytics.
  • It does not access any other person's Google account or data.

How the data is used and stored

  • Channel statistics are used only to decide which topics to make next and when to publish. They are not sold or shared with anyone.
  • The access credentials are stored privately on the owner's own computer and are never published.

Third parties

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at Google account permissions, or through the Google security settings.
